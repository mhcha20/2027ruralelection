Appendix I

Duties and Responsibilities of Returning Officers (“ROs”), Assistant Returning Officers (“AROs”) and Assistant Returning Officers (Legal) (“AROs(Legal)”)

1\. ROs are required to perform the following functions and responsibilities –

(a) To receive and process nominations of candidates.

(b) To determine the validity of nominations.

(c) To identify and allocate to candidates designated spots for display of

election advertisements.

(d) To receive hard copies of election advertisements and related

documents from candidates where appropriate.

(e) To control and monitor electioneering activities by candidates.

(f) To tender advice to candidates and their agents.

(g) To deal with enquiries and complaints pertaining to the election.

(h) To supervise the conduct of the poll.

(i) To supervise the conduct of the counting of votes.

(j) To determine the validity of questionable ballot papers.

(k) To conduct lots drawing for determining the result of an election for a

Rural Area.

(l) To declare the election results.

(m) To undertake any other duties as directed by the EAC.

2\. AROs will assist ROs in the above duties and responsibilities except items

(b), (j) and (l) which must be performed by ROs.

3\. AROs(Legal) will provide legal advice to ROs, AROs and PROs relating to

the polling issues on the polling day and counting of votes.

1\) 檢查候選人香港身分證：

□ a) 中文及英文姓名

● 檢查是否與提名表格相同。

● 候選人在填寫中文姓名時必須嚴格依照在其身分 證明文件（指候選人記錄在有關鄉村正式選民登記 冊內的資料所依據的身分證明文件，通常是其香港 身分證）上所顯示的繁體字或簡體字寫法(按情況而 定)，請參考提名表的附錄。

● 如有關名字(例如 Peter)已顯示在身分證上，應填在 中文／英文姓名一欄，而非別名一欄。

□ b) 中文別名╱英文別名

● 如果選舉主任信納某候選人通常為人所知的姓名 與其身分證明文件上所顯示的姓名有所不同，候選 人可在別名一欄中加上該姓名。

□ c) 香港身分證號碼

● 檢查是否與提名表格相同。

□ d) 出生日期 (遞交提名表格時必須年滿 21 歲)

● 請留意如候選人香港身分證上只有出生年份，在 填寫出生日期一欄時，只需填上出生年份

第二節：提名人資料

1\) 將提名人的身分證號碼輸入 RREMIS 系統，以確定以下資

料是否與提名表格所載的相同：

□ a) 中文／英文姓名

● 如提名人在提名表格上只填寫了中文姓名，沒有 填寫英文姓名(或相反情況)，而在 RREMIS 系統 上所記錄的資料(包括中文姓名、身分證號碼、主 要住址(如屬居民代表及街坊代表選舉)正確，則毋 須要求提名人補填其英文姓名。

3

● 如提名人在提名表格上的姓名與 RREMIS 系統有 出入，應要求候選人聯絡該提名人，確定提名表格 或選民登記冊中，哪一個姓名才屬正確。如是選民 登記冊出錯，請該提名人盡快填妥及交回「選民新 登記/更改登記資料申請表」，以作更正。如提名表 格出錯，該提名人必須在提名結束前作出更正。

● 如提名人不是登記選民，同時提名表格上只有 5 名提名人(包括該名並非選民的提名人)，應立即致 電總署確認其選民資格。如總署確定提名人不是 登記選民，應建議 RO/ARO 退回提名表格，並提 醒候選人在提名期結束前交回有足夠合資格的提 名人的提名表格。如提名表格上有超過 5 名提名 人，其餘提名人在初步檢查下均屬有效，可繼續處 理其提名表格。

\- 負責同事必須小心處理，特別是如該候選人只 有 5 名提名人及臨近提名期結束時間，提名人 資料錯誤可能會影響選舉主任是否接納有關 提名為有效。

□ b) 主要住址 (只適用於居民代表選舉及街坊代表選舉)

2027 RURAL ORDINARY ELECTION GUIDE ON COMPLETION OF GRID PAPER

The Home Affairs Department (HAD) will prepare a full colour Introduction to Candidates for the 2027 Rural Ordinary Election. This leaflet will carry the candidate number as shown on the ballot paper, colour photograph, name, age, occupation and email address/website of each candidate, together with a text or an illustration provided by each candidate relating to his/her electoral message or candidature. The leaflet will be sent to each elector of that Rural Area, while an enlarged copy of the Introduction to Candidates will be displayed at the polling station on the polling day for electors’ easy reference.

How to Complete the Grid Paper

2\. A candidate who wishes to have his/her particulars printed on the Introduction to Candidates1 should carefully fill in the attached grid paper in accordance with the instructions detailed below. The grid paper will be directly reproduced for printing. A candidate can only fill in the particulars on the grid paper provided by the HAD. Any photocopied or revised grid paper or anything placed outside the blue-lined area on the grid paper will NOT be accepted.

(a) The personal particulars shown on the grid paper MUST be in black, and typed, printed by computer or hand-written in clear font with a FINE ball pen. If

the grid paper is completed with a colour other than black, the colour of the relevant text will be converted to black with a poorer printing effect.

(b) The name of the candidate in both Chinese and English should be filled in (unless not applicable). Candidate may opt to provide other personal particulars (photograph, age2, occupation, email address/website and electoral message). To avoid confusion, information provided by the candidate, such as occupation and email address, might not be printed on the Introduction to Candidates if it is inconsistent with the information stated in the nomination form.

(c) The colour photograph of the candidate must be taken full face and not larger than 50mm x 40mm (2 x 1 5/8 inches) in size. The photograph must have a plain background in colour of white, light blue or light pink. Background with fluorescent or gradient colours should not be used. The photograph should be taken within the last 6 months. For better printing quality, the candidate is recommended to use photofinishing paper. Photographs printed by inkjet or laser printer should be avoided. The HAD reserves the right to adjust the image on the photograph. The photograph should be affixed to the specified position on the grid paper and another 2 identical copies of colour photographs should be submitted with the attached label supplied affixed to the back of each copy and the name of the candidate in Chinese and English (unless not applicable) and the name of the Rural Committee and Rural Area in which the candidate contests written thereon. When the candidate completes the electronic grid paper, the photograph type must be in JPEG format. If captured by scanner (600dpi), the photograph size is 50mm(H) x 40mm(W). If captured by digital camera, the image size is at least 1600px(H) x 1200px(W). Besides, the candidate should not upload the electronic grid paper captured by scanner, so as to avoid lowering the quality of the photograph.

(d) The type of information to be included inside the blue-lined area on the grid paper below the caption “Electoral Message” may include any information relating to the electoral message or candidature of the candidate, emblem and particulars of the supporting political party or organization, etc. If any emblem or particulars of the supporting political party or organization is/are included, the candidate should ensure that prior written consent of the party or organization concerned has been obtained. There is no restriction on the language, number of words, illustrations and colour to be used. However, the candidate should note that some electors may only be familiar with either the Chinese or English language and they are equally entitled to be informed of the above information. Any logo or illustrations to be included under “Electoral Message” should preferably be in line work or simple presentation for better image quality on reproduction. If the field for “Electoral Message” on the grid paper is left blank, the words “Relevant information has not been provided by the candidate” will be printed in the space provided for electoral message on the Introduction to Candidates.

(e) If the grid paper contains any content in a language other than Chinese and English, the candidate should enclose the translation of such content in Chinese or English in order to ensure that it contains materials relating only to his/her candidature of the candidate at this Election (the translation will NOT be included in the Introduction to Candidates).

(f) For enquiries, please call the enquiry hotline of the Rural Representative Election at 2152 1521.

Submission of Grid Paper

3\. The completed grid paper together with 2 additional colour photographs of the candidate which are identical to the one affixed to the grid paper (with the label supplied affixed to the back of each copy and the name of the candidate in Chinese and English and the name of the Rural Committee and Rural Area in which the candidate contests written thereon as required in paragraph (2)(c) above) should be submitted to the Returning Officer at the time of submitting the nomination form or before the end of the nomination period (i.e. on or before 5 pm, 7 December 2026). If the photographs and/or the text or illustration relating to the electoral message or candidature are not submitted before the said deadline, the relevant information will NOT be included in the Introduction to Candidates. The candidate may submit the electronic grid paper through the Rural Representative Election e-Submission Platform (https://rrevoter.had.gov.hk/remis/ESubmissionPlatformDS.jsp?lang=en). The candidate should read this Guide together with the notes and content of the grid paper, and ensure the e-Form is duly completed and signed. Also, the candidate should submit the 2 identical colour photographs as the one attached on the electronic grid paper to the Returning Officer in person by the end of nomination period.

4\. The candidate should ensure the completeness and accuracy of the information provided. The Returning Officer and the HAD will not accept any request for correction or addition to the grid paper made after the end of nomination period. The contents, nature and presentation of a candidate’s message in the Introduction to Candidates are exclusively the idea and creation of the candidate himself/herself. They will not be subject to alteration or editing by the HAD unless they are considered to be obscene, immoral, indecent, offensive, defamatory, unlawful, or contain information not relating to the promotion of the candidature of the candidate concerned.
